The Matrix

I have just met the Matrix. It resides inside M-Pesa.

You see, here's the thing. There are limits. You have your first KSh 250,000 transaction, and then another KSh 250,000. KSh 500,000 in total. And I started wondering: what happens when you take those limits and place them next to the price of a stock?

Suppose a stock is below KSh 250,000. You can buy it. There is still room. But what happens as the price starts moving up? KSh 300,000. KSh 400,000. KSh 500,000. And then beyond?

Something changes.

The Pendulum

This is where I discovered what I call The Pendulum.

The Pendulum is the window where participation is still practical for the ordinary investor. In this thought experiment, I use the M-Pesa transaction limits to illustrate that idea.

Think about it. A stock is at KSh 100,000. You are in the Pendulum. It moves to KSh 150,000, KSh 200,000, KSh 250,000. The price is moving, but you are still within the range where you can participate.

Then the Pendulum starts swinging the other way.

The stock moves to KSh 300,000, KSh 400,000, KSh 500,000. Participation becomes progressively more difficult through this payment channel. And once it moves beyond KSh 500,000, the ordinary M-Pesa buyer has been stopped out. Two KSh 250,000 transactions are no longer enough to buy one share.

You've been stopped out.

And that is where Being On The Pendulum comes in. Being On The Pendulum means you got into the stock while it was still below the lower M-Pesa transaction boundary. You got on the bus before everyone started trying to get on.

The idea isn't that M-Pesa magically determines what a stock is worth. The idea is that as the price moves, the ability of ordinary investors to participate can change with it. So perhaps the question isn't only, “How high can this stock go?” Perhaps it is also, “Am I already on the bus before it gets there?”
